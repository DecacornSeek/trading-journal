# Context-first trading framework

Date added: 2026-08-02

The web journal's pre-trade checklist now follows an 11-point context-first process: news, trade decision, gap classification, market structure, weekly VPOC, business zones, daily road map, tactical proof, cash open, emotional readiness, and risk verification.

Individual checklist answers are persisted. A new challenge trade must resolve the checklist to GO; a blocking No produces NO TRADE, and missing answers remain INCOMPLETE/CAUTION rather than being treated as passed.

## Tactical fields

Each new record can classify:

- Balance, aggressive trend, transition, breakout, reversal, or volatility regime
- News risk and opening-gap type
- Rising, falling, flat, or unclear weekly VPOC
- Business zones A and B
- Daily road map and invalidation
- Cash-open alignment
- PbD, speed, lethargy/rejection, absorption, front-running, footprint, or other proof
- Observed order-flow behavior
- Single A-to-B or fragmented execution toward B

## Evidence boundaries

The AI may test whether these labels predict outcomes once sufficient comparable records exist. It must not treat the following as proven facts from the journal:

- A universal percentage of institutional versus day-trader activity
- Institutional "panic" as the cause of every break-in move
- A fragmented approach being inherently superior
- A fixed 28% tax burden
- Kelly sizing without independent evaluation-attempt and payout data

Average cost to funded and payout expectancy require a separate record of evaluation purchases, fees, passes, funded accounts, and payouts. They are not inferred from individual trades.
